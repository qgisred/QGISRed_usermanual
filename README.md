# 🚀 Welcome to QGISRed

**QGISRed** is a powerful open access QGIS plugin designed to facilitate the construction of hydraulic models of water distribution networks, the analysis of results, the planning of improvements and the efficient management of water use through the Digital Twin

The best thing about QGISRed is that it combines the features of a **GIS Environment** with the calculation power of **EPANET**, allowing you to manage cartographic information to convert it into a hydraulic model capable of reproducing flows, pressures and water quality under any working hypothesis. In addition, powered by field data, it also allows the real state of the network to be reproduced through a digital twin.

### Why use QGISRed?

* **Hydraulic analysis and water quality tool.** QGISRed fully reproduces and extends the calculation capabilities of EPANET up to the latest version 2.3. It is a perfect environment for training in network hydraulics
* **Full georeferencing**: Take advantage of all the advantages of QGIS to geolocate network elements, and superimpose them on any cartographic background, as well as to interact with said backgrounds.
* **Simple Data Management**: Allows you to import INP files and SHP files, georeferenced or not, with a simple and intuitive relational structure. The QGISRed data model is open and allows interoperability with other applications.
* **Specialized Tools**: Includes utilities for network debugging, automatic assignment of lengths, elevations and roughness, assignment of demands to nodes, scenario management and complete analysis of the results without leaving QGIS.
* **Digital Twins**: Supports additional elements such as connections, manual valves and meters for an advanced level of detail. The connection of these elements with field data allows us to reproduce the real mode of behavior of the network.&#x20;
* **From the academic world to the professional:** From building simple diagrams of hydraulic systems, to modeling reality to plan actions aimed at improving hydraulic performance, energy efficiency or water quality, to monitoring the behavior of the network based on real data for decision making.
