# 🧬 Digital Twin

![Digital Twin Menu](../assets/images/image15.png)

Turn your GIS model into an advanced Digital Twin by incorporating elements that go beyond traditional hydraulic modeling.

### Digital Twin Capabilities:
* [Connections and Counters](connections.md): Models the connection with the end client.
* [Sensors and Remote Reading](sensors.md): Integrate data in real time (SCADA) with your model.
* **Cut-Off Valves**: Manages the manual closure of sectors and detects customer conditions.

---
> ❗ **IMPORTANT**:
> You can upload automatic meter readings in **Series** or **Table** formats for maximum accuracy in demands.
